# Product Requirements Document — Travel Planning App

## Product Goal
Create a simple AI-assisted travel planning experience that helps a traveler organize a trip from initial planning through the trip itself, while keeping important travel information in one usable place.

## Product Principles
- Easy enough for a nontechnical traveler.
- Useful before and during a trip.
- Reduce scattered travel information and repeated manual planning.
- Let AI assist with organization and recommendations while keeping the traveler in control.
- Learn from a real trip before overbuilding.

## Initial Validation Scenario
The first end-to-end test will be a Texas trip for the project owner and his daughter. The scenario will be used to discover missing requirements, remove unnecessary features, and validate the workflow.

## MVP Capabilities
1. Create and manage a trip.
2. Store destinations and trip dates.
3. Maintain travelers associated with a trip.
4. Build an itinerary organized by day.
5. Track lodging, transportation, activities, reservations, and useful notes.
6. Maintain trip tasks/checklists.
7. Keep important confirmation/reference information accessible.
8. Provide AI-assisted planning and itinerary organization.
9. Support changes to plans without rebuilding the entire trip.
10. Present a practical trip view suitable for use while traveling.

## Later / Optional Capabilities
- Voice interaction.
- Automated extraction from connected sources.
- Proactive reminders and travel alerts.
- Collaboration with additional travelers.
- Expense/budget tools.
- Deeper recommendation and booking integrations.

These are not automatically part of the MVP and should be promoted into scope only after validation.

## Success Criteria
The MVP succeeds when the Texas trip can be planned and managed end-to-end in the application and the owner can identify what should be added, changed, or removed based on actual use.

## Open Product Decisions
Detailed UX, provider integrations, automation boundaries, and monetization remain subject to owner review during iterative testing.
